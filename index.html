Meet Uncle Sweet

Uncle Sweet wasn't born — he crystallized. One day a sugar vat bubbled a little too hard, and out climbed a man made entirely of warmth, generosity, and an unreasonable amount of body mass.

He opened this candy shop so that every child, adult, and mildly concerned passerby could experience joy in its densest, chunkiest form. Every treat here is hand-rolled by Uncle Sweet himself, using recipes he claims came to him in a "sugar dream."
